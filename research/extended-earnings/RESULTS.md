## Reading
Going into a report extended does not make a drop more likely or bigger: half
of extended reports go down the next day, the same as the rest, and the
average is flat. (The "reached the 50-day" row is lower for extended stocks
because they start farther from it — the pre-set check was always going to
favour the not-extended group; the other four checks fail without it.) The
most stretched names move more **both ways** — at NOW's level, 22% fell 5% or
more next day, but the best ten rose 28–42% — which is volatility, the thing
the options already price before the report. NOW's own extended reports rose
more often than they fell. Consequence as pre-registered: recorded; NOW's
drop stays one case. Whether a pre-report put pays is still the unanswered
options question (no historical option prices).
