## 5. Why it worked — hypothesis

Most likely: **structural premise held + path-of-least-resistance.** Three independent signals lined up at entry:

1. Large put wall at $705 (−$316.5M GEX)
2. NOTABLE directional flow into $700 + $705 puts at 7–8× OI
3. Dealer short-delta at $705 (DEX = −$2.1M)

Spot at entry was $706.19 — essentially AT the put wall. In Regime B (negative net GEX), dealers sell dips, which is exactly the dynamic that pulls spot down to and through the put wall. The 5-wide spread was sized to capture the move from $705 to $700; spot only needed to push 1 strike below entry to start filling out the spread.

**Caveat:** Same-day-expiration puts have extreme convexity. A +92.8% intraday move on a 0DTE bear put can come from spot moving from $706.19 to $702 just as easily as from $704. Without intraday `gex_history` snapshots we can't say with confidence "the wall did the work" vs "spot moved and the math worked."

What we **can** say: the entry conditions matched textbook Regime-B-pin-to-put-wall setup, the thesis was specific, and you took it within 4 minutes of the LLM seeing the matrix. That's discipline.
